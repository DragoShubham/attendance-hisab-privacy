Privacy Policy for Attendance Hisab

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Attendance Hisab ("the App") is a workforce attendance, wage and payroll management application provided by Shubham Kumar ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and delete information when you use Attendance Hisab.

Important: Attendance Hisab is intended for workforce recordkeeping. It is not a bank, lender, investment service, insurance service, or government application.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

Depending on the sign-in method used by the App, we may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, account identifier, and authentication information needed to provide your account.

Workforce information

If you use the App to manage workers, we may collect information you enter about workers, such as their name, phone number, worker type, wage or salary, overtime settings, joining date, project/site information, and notes.

Attendance and payroll information

We may collect and store information you enter or generate through the App, including attendance status, attendance dates, hours worked, overtime, wages, advances, payments, deductions, bonuses, payroll records, and wage summaries.

Reminders and app settings

The App may store your reminder preferences, such as whether daily attendance reminders are enabled and the selected reminder time. Notification permission may be requested by Android when required.

Device and technical information

We may receive limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App, such as app version, operating system information, and diagnostic information, where supported by the services used by the App.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Provide attendance, wage, overtime, advance, payment, and payroll features.
  • Synchronize your data across supported sessions or devices.
  • Provide worker and contractor management features.
  • Send attendance reminders when you enable them.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the App.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the App, such as authentication, hosting, database, cloud synchronization, analytics, security, or technical infrastructure providers, where applicable. Such providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to us and subject to appropriate contractual or security protections.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of users or the service.

4. Worker and Payroll Privacy

Workforce and payroll information is intended to be accessible only to the account and authorized users to whom the information belongs. Knowing a worker's phone number alone should not provide access to that worker's private payroll information.

Users are responsible for entering accurate information and for using worker-management and sharing features only when they have an appropriate reason and authorization to do so.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Where supported by the services used by the App, data may be protected in transit using encryption and access controls.

No internet or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and workforce information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App and its features, maintain legitimate business and security records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Account Deletion and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Attendance Hisab account through the account deletion feature provided in the App or by contacting us at the email address below.

When an account deletion request is completed, we will delete the account and associated personal information that we are required and able to delete, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate obligations. Where data must be retained, it will be retained only for the applicable purpose and period.

8. Children's Privacy

Attendance Hisab is a general workforce/business application and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may rely on third-party infrastructure or services to provide authentication, cloud storage, notifications, analytics, or other functionality. Their handling of information may be governed by their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review the privacy practices of relevant third-party services.

10. Your Choices

  • You can review and update information available through your account and App settings.
  • You can disable attendance reminders through the App settings or device notification settings.
  • You can request account deletion as described above.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
