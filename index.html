Privacy Policy for Attendance Hisab

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Attendance Hisab ("the App") is an offline-first workforce attendance, wage, and payroll management application for Android provided by Shubham Kumar (operating as Drago Shubham, "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains transparently how we collect, use, store, synchronize, protect, and delete information when you use Attendance Hisab (Application ID: com.drago.attendancehisab).

Important: Attendance Hisab is a private business recordkeeping utility intended for contractors, small business owners, and supervisors. It is not a bank, financial institution, lender, money transmitter, investment platform, insurance provider, or government service.
Looking to Delete Your Account or Associated Data?
You have full control over your information. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud and local data either directly inside the app or online without needing the app installed. Visit our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Request Page →

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect and process data necessary to provide workforce recordkeeping, secure authentication, cloud synchronization, and in-app advertising:

A. Account & Authentication Information (Firebase Authentication)

To secure your account and synchronize records across devices, the App uses Google Firebase Phone Authentication:

  • Mobile Phone Number: Collected when you sign in or register to deliver a one-time verification code (SMS OTP).
  • Firebase User Identifier (UID): A unique, random internal account identifier assigned by Firebase Authentication (e.g. users/{uid}) to associate and isolate your workforce records.
  • We do not collect or store account passwords. Phone sign-in is passwordless and authenticated directly via SMS OTP.

B. Workforce & Contractor Information (Stored Locally & Cloud Firestore)

Information you voluntarily enter to manage your staff and workers:

  • Worker profiles: Worker name, contact phone number, worker type (daily wage or monthly fixed salary), standard daily working hours, and custom overtime rules.
  • Project / site details: Worksite names and project references used to categorize attendance.
  • Contractor profile: Optional business name and contractor contact name displayed on wage statements.

C. Attendance, Advances & Payroll Records (Stored Locally & Cloud Firestore)

Records created and managed during day-to-day operations:

  • Daily attendance: Attendance status (Present, Half-day, Absent), attendance date, and decimal overtime hours.
  • Financial transactions: Cash advances given, periodic payments or wage settlements disbursed, dates, payment modes, and operational notes.
  • Payroll computations: System-calculated monthly gross earnings, total overtime earnings, advance deductions, and final net balance payable.

D. Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob SDK)

To support the free availability of the application, Attendance Hisab integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display banner and interstitial advertisements. When advertisements are requested or displayed, the Google AdMob SDK may automatically collect certain device and diagnostic information as governed by Google's privacy standards, including:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android devices. Users can reset or delete this identifier at any time in Android settings.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse/approximate geographic location inferred from IP address to deliver relevant advertising.
  • Device & Network Diagnostics: Device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, network carrier, radio type (Wi-Fi/cellular), and screen specifications.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding ad impressions, user clicks, ad load durations, and crash/diagnostic logs for ad performance and fraud prevention.

Note: Attendance Hisab does NOT collect fine GPS location, contacts lists, camera images, microphone audio, financial account credentials, or biometric data.

E. Reminders & Notification Settings

If you enable daily attendance reminders, your preferred reminder time is stored locally on your device. The App requests the standard Android notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) solely to schedule local device notifications reminding you to mark attendance.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Account Management: Authenticating your identity and maintaining your user account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Core Functionality: Storing, calculating, and managing worker attendance, overtime hours, advances, and payroll balances.
  • Offline-First Synchronization: Caching records locally on your device and automatically synchronizing them with Cloud Firestore when an internet connection is available.
  • Ad Serving: Serving banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob in compliance with Google advertising policies.
  • Statement Generation: Creating formatted wage summaries that you can review, download, or share voluntarily.
  • Reminders: Delivering local push reminders when scheduled by you.
  • Customer Support & Compliance: Responding to user inquiries, resolving technical issues, and fulfilling account or data deletion requests.

  • This feature is completely voluntary and initiated solely by you.
  • When you tap "Share via WhatsApp" or "Share", the App generates a plain-text statement and passes it to WhatsApp (wa.me) or the Android system share chooser.
  • Once information is transmitted outside of the App, it leaves our systems and is governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy (WhatsApp Legal Terms) and the recipient's privacy practices. We encourage you to ensure you have worker consent before sharing wage details over messaging channels.

C. Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, regulatory mandate, or valid governmental authority, or when necessary to protect the legal rights, safety, or security of our users or the public.

4. Worker and Payroll Privacy

Attendance Hisab is built with tenant isolation:

  • All workforce and payroll data in Cloud Firestore is strictly isolated under your unique user ID (users/{uid}/...).
  • Cloud Firestore security rules enforce that only the authenticated user owning that account can read or write their records.
  • Workers cannot view contractor records simply by knowing a phone number.
  • Contractors and employers are responsible for ensuring that worker details entered into the App are accurate and handled in compliance with local workplace regulations.

5. Data Security

We employ multi-layered technical safeguards designed to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the App, Firebase servers, and AdMob servers are secured using industry-standard TLS 1.3/HTTPS encryption.
  • Strict Database Rules: Cloud Firestore security rules strictly enforce authentication checks (request.auth.uid == userId), preventing unauthorized read or write access from other users.
  • Local Device Sandboxing: Offline data is stored in device application storage protected by the Android operating system application sandbox.

While we implement rigorous security measures, no electronic transmission or digital storage method is 100% impenetrable. We encourage users to maintain device lock protection (PIN, fingerprint, or password) on their phones.

6. Data Retention

We retain your workforce, attendance, and payroll records for as long as your account remains active and you choose to maintain them. You can edit or delete individual workers, attendance entries, or transactions at any time within the App.

If you choose to delete your account, your data will be permanently purged in accordance with our deletion policy outlined in Section 7.

7. Account Deletion and Data Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your Attendance Hisab account and all associated personal and workforce data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

  1. In-App Deletion (Immediate): Open Attendance Hisab → navigate to Settings → scroll to Delete Account → type DELETE to confirm → tap Confirm Delete.
  2. Web / Email Request (Without Reinstalling): If you have uninstalled the App or cannot access your device, you can request full account and data deletion online through our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Request Page or by emailing us at dragoking.dk0@gmail.com with your registered phone number. We process verified requests within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted

When account deletion is completed, our systems permanently and irreversibly erase:

  • Your authentication profile and registered phone number from Firebase Authentication.
  • Your master user document in Cloud Firestore (users/{uid}).
  • All worker rosters and profiles (users/{uid}/workers).
  • All daily attendance logs and overtime records (users/{uid}/attendance).
  • All cash advance entries (users/{uid}/advances).
  • All payment and settlement records (users/{uid}/payments).
  • All monthly payroll summaries and calculations (users/{uid}/payrolls).
  • All worksite and project categories (users/{uid}/projects).
  • All local cached data, pending synchronization queues, and application preferences on the device.

Legitimate Exceptions to Deletion

In accordance with privacy regulations and technical realities, the following exceptions apply:

  • Externally Exported Files: Any CSV spreadsheets or wage summary files that you previously exported to your device storage or Google Drive cannot be deleted by our server; you must delete these local files manually from your device.
  • Messages Sent Externally: Any wage slips or messages shared via WhatsApp or SMS remain on the recipient's device and WhatsApp servers according to their respective policies.
  • Transient Security Logs: Temporary technical logs generated by Google Cloud infrastructure for security monitoring, DDoS prevention, and fraud detection expire automatically in accordance with standard Google Cloud retention schedules.
  • Employer Statutory Obligations: Contractors and business owners may have independent statutory obligations under local labor laws to retain wage registers; deleting your Attendance Hisab account clears our digital systems but does not alter your statutory recordkeeping obligations.
