Account & Data Deletion Request

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Application: Attendance Hisab (Workforce Attendance, Wages & Payroll)

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global data privacy standards, users of Attendance Hisab have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal and workforce data at any time.

You do not need to have the app currently installed on your phone to exercise this right. Below are the two official methods for deleting your account and data.

Warning — Permanent Action:
Account deletion is irreversible. Once processed, your account credentials, cloud synchronization, worker profiles, attendance history, cash advance records, and payroll computations are permanently purged and cannot be recovered.

Method 1: Request Deletion via Email (Without Reinstalling the App)

If you have already uninstalled the application, do not have access to your original Android device, or prefer to submit a deletion request directly, you can submit a deletion request by email:

Direct Email Deletion Request

Send an email to: dragoking.dk0@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - Attendance Hisab

Required Details to Include:

  • Your registered 10-digit mobile phone number (including country code, e.g. +91-XXXXXXXXXX) used to sign into Attendance Hisab via Firebase Phone Authentication.
  • A clear statement requesting the deletion of your account and all associated records.

Verification & Processing Timeline:

  • To protect your account from unauthorized deletion requests, our team will send a verification SMS or email to confirm account ownership.
  • Once ownership is verified, all cloud records and authentication entries are completely purged within 7 business days.
  • You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data deletion has been completed.

Method 2: Immediate In-App Deletion

If you have the Attendance Hisab app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account and data immediately in real-time:

  1. Open the Attendance Hisab app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Security section.
  4. Tap the red Delete Account button.
  5. A safety modal will appear. To prevent accidental deletion, type DELETE into the confirmation field.
  6. Tap Confirm Delete.

The app will instantly execute cloud and local cleanup routines, purge your Firestore collections, delete your Firebase Authentication user account, wipe local device storage, and return you to the initial sign-in screen.

What Data Is Permanently Deleted

Regardless of whether you request deletion in-app or via email, our deletion procedure permanently and completely erases:

  • Authentication Credentials: Your registered phone number and user record in Google Firebase Authentication.
  • User Profile: Your master contractor profile document in Cloud Firestore (users/{uid}).
  • Worker Rosters: All worker names, phone numbers, wage rates, and overtime configurations (users/{uid}/workers).
  • Attendance Logs: All daily attendance entries (Present, Half-day, Absent) and overtime hours recorded across all dates (users/{uid}/attendance).
  • Cash Advances: All advance payments, notes, and transaction timestamps (users/{uid}/advances).
  • Payouts & Settlements: All recorded wage payments and settlements (users/{uid}/payments).
  • Monthly Payrolls: All computed wage statements and month-end settlement totals (users/{uid}/payrolls).
  • Project Sites: All project / worksite categorizations (users/{uid}/projects).
  • Local Device Storage: All offline records, cached data, and pending synchronization queues in device storage.

Legitimate Exceptions & Non-Deletable Data

The following legitimate exceptions apply to the deletion process in accordance with technological limits and legal standards:

  • External CSV / Spreadsheet Files: If you previously downloaded or exported payroll/attendance CSV files to your device storage or Google Drive, those files reside outside our servers and must be deleted manually by you from your device.
  • Messages Shared via WhatsApp: Wage statements or summaries previously sent to workers or third parties through WhatsApp or SMS remain on the recipient's phone and on WhatsApp's network. Attendance Hisab has no ability or authorization to access or alter third-party messaging services.
  • Transient Cloud Security Logs: Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure generates brief technical server logs (such as IP addresses and connection timestamps) strictly for DDoS mitigation, abuse prevention, and network security. These logs expire and purge automatically in accordance with standard Google Cloud retention schedules.
  • Employer Statutory Compliance: Under Indian labor and commercial regulations, employers and contractors are independently responsible for maintaining official statutory muster rolls and payment records. Deleting your Attendance Hisab account clears our digital systems but does not alter your statutory obligations.
